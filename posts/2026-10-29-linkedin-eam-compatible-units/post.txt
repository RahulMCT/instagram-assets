A utility estimator shouldn't have to rebuild "install one pole with a transformer" from scratch for every job.

That's what Compatible Units in HxGN EAM are for. I put the whole flow on one cheat sheet.

Here's how it works:
✅ The CU Library holds standard templates of labor, parts, tools and services, and child CUs let you build assemblies
✅ A CU Design Project can hold many CU Estimates, one for each design option
✅ Each estimate places CUs on work points or spans with an action: Install, Remove, Abandon or Transfer
✅ Perform Estimation costs only the lines that match that action, so you get a different estimate for an install than for a removal
✅ Approve a Design estimate, click Generate WO Activities, and EAM fills in Plan Labor, Parts, Plan Tools and a material list on the work order

Two lessons from configuring it:
1. Give every part line an action type. Labor and tools with a blank action are costed for any action, but a part with a blank action is skipped.
2. Set the CU standard rates and prices before anyone estimates, or the totals mean nothing.

On top of that, accounting and fixed asset codes produce a FERC Unit Estimate, and overhead codes can add a surcharge by customer type.
