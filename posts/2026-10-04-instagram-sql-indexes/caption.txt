Indexes are not “make my query fast” magic. They’re a trade: faster reads, more work on every write.

An index is a sorted lookup structure (usually a B-Tree) that points to rows so the engine can seek instead of scanning the whole table. Selectivity, composite left-prefix order, and covering indexes decide whether it actually helps. Always verify with EXPLAIN — don’t guess.
