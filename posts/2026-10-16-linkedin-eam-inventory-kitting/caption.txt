The fastest work order is the one where the technician walks out with everything already in one box.

That is exactly what Inventory Kitting in HxGN EAM is for: pre-staging a set of parts and assets as one kit, while keeping lot tracking and shelf life under control.

A few things I always check when setting it up:

1. Organization Options first. KITLDESC and KITPPULD set Build Kit defaults, LOTIEXPL = NO blocks issuing expired lots, and ISSTOEMP decides whether parts can go to employees (YES / NO / KITS ONLY).
2. Track as Kit can't be ticked if the part already has transactions, sits on a requisition/PO/RFQ/blanket order, is on another kit template, or has a preferred supplier. Plan kit part codes up front.
3. Once ticked, EAM forces Track by Lot and Prevent Reorders and sets Tracking Method to Stock. Kits are built from stock, never purchased.
4. Build Kit uses the Kit Template as a roadmap (Retrieve Parts, pick bin/lot/asset, Build Kit). Breakup Kit brings it back: return to store, move to a repair bin, or mark as used.
5. The Employee screen's Outstanding Issues tab shows what is still out with whom.
