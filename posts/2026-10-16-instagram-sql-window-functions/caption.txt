"Find the 2nd highest salary in each department" — if this SQL question makes you freeze, this cheat-sheet is for you.

Window functions calculate across a group of related rows WITHOUT collapsing them like GROUP BY does. You add OVER(PARTITION BY ... ORDER BY ...) and every row keeps its details plus a rank, running total or previous-row value.

ROW_NUMBER is always unique, RANK leaves gaps after ties (1,2,2,4), DENSE_RANK doesn't (1,2,2,3). And remember: you can't filter a window function in WHERE, so wrap it in a CTE first.
