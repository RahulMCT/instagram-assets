A road is not one asset. It's 15 miles of asset, and the pothole is at mile 6.

That's the problem Linear Equipment solves in HxGN EAM (now Octave Attune EAM). Roads, rail tracks and pipelines get a length, and work orders can target just a slice of it.

How I set it up:

1. Give the asset an Equipment Length, a Linear Reference UOM and a From Point. A 10-mile highway starting at marker 65 then shows mile 1 as 66.
2. Pick a Linear Equipment Type (Right of Way, Route, Segment) together with a Direction. Leave both blank and you lose offsets and route tracing.
3. Add features on the Linear References tab: mileposts, points of interest, related equipment like bus stops, or related parts like ties and gravel. Repeat Every Length creates every milepost in one save.
4. On the work order, set From/To Point or pick a reference point with an offset, e.g. 25 ft north of the milepost.
5. For big jobs, Trace Route crosses over between routes through branch points and creates child or MEC work orders, by route or by segment (TRACELEV).

My tip: predefine linear references on the equipment Class. They are copied to every new road or track automatically.
