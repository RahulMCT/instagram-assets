Most EAM integrations I see still hard-code their endpoints. OData in HxGN EAM lets the client discover them instead.

I put the whole OData setup from the HxGN EAM 12.3 brief on one cheat sheet.

Here's how it works:
✅ The Service document at /axis/odataservices lists every supported entity, and $metadata gives each entity's properties and key fields. Neither call needs credentials.
✅ Six operations map to HTTP verbs: GET for one record or a collection, POST to add, PUT to sync the full record, PATCH for partial updates, and DELETE
✅ Keys go in the URL, like Lots(LOTCODE='L1',ORGANIZATIONCODE='ORG1'). Strings take quotes, numbers don't.
✅ Get Collection supports $filter, $count, $top and $skip, and it returns rows from the entity's default Dataspy
✅ Headers carry tenant, organization, and parentid (only for tab entities like Task Plan Checklists)

Three gotchas from real testing:
1. Dates need YEAR as YYYY. Epoch milliseconds aren't supported.
2. $filter doesn't work yet on DATETIME, QUANTITY or AMOUNT fields.
3. A # in a code must be sent as %23.

The 12.3 brief lists Assets, Positions, Systems, Nonconformities, Case Management, Lots and Task Plans, plus some of their tabs.
