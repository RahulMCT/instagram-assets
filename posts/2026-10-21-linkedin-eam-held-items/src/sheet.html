📦 HxGN EAM Held Items CHEAT SHEET
Receive parts for a specific work order straight to the WO or to a held-item bin, issue them as packages, and move them between held and stock bins. (Infor EAM Held Items brief, 11.6.1)

1. What is a held item?

  • A part received for a work order instead of into a normal stock bin
  • Skips middle receiving steps = better productivity
  • Received direct to WO or to a held-item bin
  • Issued / returned as a package

2. Organization Options

OptionValues (default)
PDRCPTTOWO / HELD (WO): Direct to WO ticked or not by default
HELDBINSOPTIONAL / PROTECTED / REQUIRED (PROTECTED): Bin field on direct-purchase receipt

3. Stores › Bins tab

  • Each bin: For Stock ✔ and/or For Held Items ✔
  • For Stock is ticked by default
  • Any combination is allowed

4. PO Receipts › Active Lines

  • Direct Materials line → Direct to WO ✔ + WO–Activity filled
  • Direct to WO ✔ = received to the WO
  • Direct to WO ✘ = received to the store as a Held Item
  • Bin field follows HELDBINS

5. Non-PO Receipts › Parts

  • Fill Hold for Work Order–Activity = held item
  • Bin field then follows HELDBINS
  • Clear it and Bin goes back to Required

6. Packages

  • Status: Unfinished → Approved (or Canceled)
  • Issue/Return Parts on Approved = Issue Mode, status becomes Issued
  • On Issued = Return Mode (Fill / Clear Return Qty.)
  • Issued To (employee) required; Parts + Transactions tabs

7. Three transfer screens

ScreenMoves
Held Items Transferheld bin → held bin (same or other store)
Held Items to Stock Transferheld bin → stock bin
Stock to Held Items Transferstock bin → held bin (WO–Activity required)

8. Labels & transfer rules

  • Label Templates for each held-item transaction on Stores and Parts › Stores tab
  • Parts › Stores tab is checked first, then the Store
  • Header: Description, From/To Store, Default To Bin
  • Track by Asset part → Transfer Qty. fixed at 1

9a. Held item flow

PO / Non-PO receipt→Held bin (for WO)→Package Approved→Issued→Return / to stock

9b. Consultant tips

  1. Decide PDRCPTTO with stores team before go-live
  2. Create dedicated held-item bins
  3. Set held-item label templates per store
  4. Move leftovers back with Held Items to Stock

9c. Key points

  • Direct to WO ✘ = held
  • HELDBINS drives Bin
  • Approved → Issued
