Most people say “JavaScript is single-threaded”… and then freeze when asked why Promise.then runs before setTimeout(0).

That’s the Event Loop.

One call stack. Async work finishes in the host (browser/Node). Callbacks wait in queues. Microtasks (Promises) drain before the next macrotask (timers). That’s why the order is A → D → C → B in today’s sheet.
