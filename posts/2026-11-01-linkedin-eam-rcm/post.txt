Most maintenance teams still rank risk by gut feel. RCM in HxGN EAM turns it into a number you can defend.

I put the full Reliability Centered Maintenance setup from the Infor EAM RCM brief on one cheat sheet.

Here's how it fits together:
✅ Each RCM Project (System Codes entity STPR) gets its own Risk Matrix: Consequence Categories, Probabilities and Risk Levels
✅ Risk Priority Number = Probability Rating × Consequence Severity, and the highest RPN on the RCM tab sets the equipment's Risk Level
✅ Three RCM Levels: Risk Assessment for lines and locations, Risk Analysis (adds Failure Modes) for lower-level equipment, and FMECA
✅ The Failure Mitigation tab links Failure Modes to PM Schedules, Maintenance Patterns or PM Plans, and Activate Mitigation attaches the equipment for you
✅ Black Swan flags low-probability, high-cost events that would otherwise never drive the RPN

Two gotchas from projects:
1. Changing the Risk Matrix doesn't trickle down to existing assessments. Re-check the RCM tabs.
2. Imported mitigation from an RCM Template always arrives with Mitigation Applied unticked.

Then the Equipment at Risk and Potential Yearly Savings reports show where to add PMs and where you may be over-maintaining.
