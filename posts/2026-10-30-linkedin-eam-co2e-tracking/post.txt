Your utility bills already hold half of your carbon report. HxGN EAM can turn them into CO2e numbers.

I put the whole CO2e tracking setup from the Infor EAM Asset Sustainability brief on one cheat sheet.

Here's how it works:
✅ The Greenhouse Gases screen stores each gas with its ratio to CO2 (methane 21:1, SF6 23,900:1 in the brief), and the CO2EUOM install parameter sets the unit, such as lbs.
✅ For Scope-2, you define Electricity as a commodity, record the utility's fuel mix on the Supplier, and enter the gases each fuel emits per MWH
✅ Equipment counts only when both Utility Bill Level and GAS Tracked are checked
✅ Enter the utility bill on a Non-PO invoice voucher, approve it, and the nightly GDRV process updates the CO2e tables
✅ For Scope-1, tick Leak when a refrigerant part is issued to refill a system, and that leaked gas is counted

Two lessons for consultants:
1. Keep the Fuel Mix date-effective, because the utility's mix changes over time.
2. Train the stores team on the Leak check box. If nobody ticks it, your Scope-1 number stays at zero.

Three reports then show CO2e by gas, by Scope category, and by equipment.
