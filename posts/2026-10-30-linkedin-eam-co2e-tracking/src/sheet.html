🌱 HxGN EAM CO2e Tracking CHEAT SHEET
Turn utility bills and refrigerant leaks into carbon reporting, right inside EAM. (Infor EAM Carbon Dioxide Equivalence brief, 11.6.1)

1. What is CO2e?

  • "Carbon dioxide equivalent": the common unit for global warming potential (GWP)
  • CO2 is the reference gas (1 : 1)
  • Every other gas gets a ratio to CO2
  • Needs Asset Sustainability Edition + its CD key

4. Basic setup (Scope-2)

  • Greenhouse Gases screen: gas + CO2e ratio
  • Unit of Measure: e.g. lbs.
  • Install param CO2EUOM = unit for CO2e
  • Commodities: Electricity, MWH, Scope-2
  • System Codes FLTP: fuel types (Coal, Nuclear)

5. Supplier + fuels

  • Supplier Commodity tab: utility supplies Electricity
  • Fuel Mix tab: capacity per fuel → % mix (Date Effective)
  • Commodity Fuels tab: gas emitted per UOM of each fuel
Coal 1,000 MWH/day ≈ 16.7%
Nuclear 5,000 MWH/day ≈ 83.3%

6. Equipment flags

  • Facility Details: Utility Bill Level + GAS Tracked
  • Both must be checked to count in CO2e
  • Design Consumption tab: load ranges + rates, Default Load Factor (0–100)
0–29% 0.28 MWH/day
30–85% 0.22 MWH/day
86–100% 0.35 MWH/day

7. Daily operation

  • Invoice Voucher: Type Non-PO, Status Unfinished
  • Utility Bill tab: source equipment, commodity, dates, usage, cost
  • Set voucher to Approved
  • Nightly process GDRV updates CO2e tables
  • Actual Consumption tab can override the Load Factor

8. Scope-1 fugitive gas

  • Part (e.g. refrigerant ABC-125) Greenhouse Gases tab: gas + emission per UOM
  • Part marked as Fugitive Gas
  • Issue/Return Parts: tick Leak on refill
  • The leaked quantity is counted as CO2e

9. The math + the reports

CO2e = gas emitted × CO2e ratio
e.g. SF6: 0.002 lb/MWH × 23,900 = 47.8 lb CO2e per MWH

10b. Consultant tips

  1. Check both equipment flags
  2. Keep Fuel Mix dated
  3. Train storemen on Leak
  4. Confirm GDRV runs nightly

10c. Key points

  • Bills become emissions
  • Leaks become Scope-1
